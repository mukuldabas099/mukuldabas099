## 🔬 Research

### [Resource-Aware CoMesh](https://github.com/mukuldabas099/Resource-Aware-CoMesh) · May – Sep 2026
Extended CoMesh with five resource-aware protocols, including energy-aware leader election, predicate push-down, and sleep-aware membership. In simulation:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Leader re-elections | **18.1%** fewer |
| Fairness (Gini) | **23.8%** better |
| Bandwidth | up to **99.2%** reduction |
